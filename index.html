How it works

Five steps. Next refill after six months.

Set it up once and Ice Cap Pro is ready whenever a headache, migraine, or stressful day calls for it.

1

Open

Access the filling ports hidden under the cap's upper layer.

2

Fill

Add water to about 85% of each port using the included funnel.

3

Close

Seal each port tightly with its velcro cover to prevent leaks.

4

Freeze

Lay it flat in the freezer until the water turns to ice.

5

Wear

Adjust the strap and elastic base for a snug, comfortable fit.

Inside the design

Four ports. One even, steady cool.

All four filling ports sit hidden beneath the cap's upper layer and close securely with velcro, so water stays exactly where you put it.
